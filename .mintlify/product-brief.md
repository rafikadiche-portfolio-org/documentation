# Product brief

## Description
The public technical profile behind rafikadiche.com: what Rafik Adiche uses and how, with each claim linked to a live project where it can be checked.

## Primary audience
Two readers at once: recruiters and clients who scan in under a minute, and technical evaluators who follow the links to check a claim.

## Jobs to be done
- See the role, stack and availability at a glance.
- Open a project, its live app and its own documentation.
- Check a skill or pattern against the project that shows it.

## Motivation
The code repositories are private, so this site is where the work can be verified. It stays at the "patterns and public API" level: no resource identifiers, access policies or runbooks.
